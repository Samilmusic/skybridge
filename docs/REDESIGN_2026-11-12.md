# Sky Bridge visual and navigation redesign

The homepage now presents three customer tasks: visa assistance, UAE experiences and business solutions. Each task has a direct route. The former decorative analytics/dashboard hero has been replaced with destination photography and a plain description of the available services.

## Changes

- New cream, deep-green and lime visual system, with stronger typography and editorial image composition.
- Consistent desktop navigation and a mobile menu on public service, destination, company and experience pages.
- Searchable destination cards, with a shorter initial list and an explicit “show all” control.
- Accessible business-service tabs with arrow-key navigation and service-specific links.
- Clear contact form, fixed help link and a consistent footer.
- Scroll reveals, slow photo motion, a route animation, hover transitions and subtle pointer movement. Reduced-motion preferences disable movement and preserve content visibility.
- Dedicated responsive layouts for narrow phones, tablets and desktop. Inputs use legible mobile font sizes.
- Duplicate initial headings in the visa form are reduced, bringing the actual fields closer to the page introduction.
- Arrow symbols that did not render reliably are replaced with inline SVG icons.
- Existing Supabase enquiries, private visa submission and Resend notifications remain connected. No catalogue prices, customer records or roles were changed for this visual work.

## Verification

Browser-rendered frames checked at 320, 390 and 768 CSS px. Their content widths are 305, 375 and 753 px after the browser's vertical scrollbar. The homepage, experience catalogue, experience form and visa form had matching document/client widths; the tested forms had no inputs outside the viewport. The Google Ads page was also checked at the narrow phone width.

The mobile menu opens and exposes all three service routes. Destination search returns the matching destination, clearing search restores the initial list, and “show all” exposes all ten destinations. Business tabs change the visible service panel. The standalone UAE visa form still initializes. Static checks cover local links, fragment targets, unique IDs and JavaScript syntax.

These are browser viewport checks, not a claim of physical-device or every-browser certification. The catalogue content gaps documented in the deep audit are separate from this design work.
