A practical delivery process.

  1. Discover: agree users, tasks, current systems and success measures.
  2. Design: review the key journeys and proposed screens before full implementation.
  3. Build: deliver the scoped functionality with appropriate data access and error handling.
  4. Validate: test the agreed journeys on desktop and mobile, then resolve findings.
  5. Launch: confirm account ownership, deployment responsibilities and handover.

Before you commission a build.

What is included in the quotation?

Pages or modules, integrations, content responsibilities, milestones, revisions, testing, handover and support. Hosting, paid software and third-party charges are identified separately.

Can you work with an existing website?

Yes. Share the current site and the problem you want fixed. We review the existing implementation before recommending a scoped improvement or replacement.

How are data and access handled?

Roles, required integrations and personal-data flows are reviewed during discovery. Server secrets stay on the server, and staff should receive only the access their role requires.
