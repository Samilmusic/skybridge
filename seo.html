DELIVERABLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Know what your project includes.

Technical discovery audit

Identify crawl, indexing, rendering and internal-linking issues on the agreed site. Prioritize fixes by likely effect and implementation effort.

Search and content architecture

Group relevant searches by customer intent and map them to useful pages. Create an editorial plan with responsibilities for factual review and publication.

On-page implementation

Improve titles, headings, page structure, internal links and relevant structured information. Content should answer a customer’s question rather than repeat keywords.

Organic performance review

Track agreed discovery and engagement measures and review qualified enquiries where measurement is available. Organic improvements are assessed over time.

BEFORE WE START

Common questions.

How quickly will SEO produce results?

There is no fixed timeline or guaranteed ranking. The proposal separates immediate technical work from longer-term content and visibility goals.

What do you need from us?

Share your business website, target market, objectives, current setup and any constraints. Access is requested only for the accounts needed for the agreed scope.

When can work start, and what does it cost?

After the discovery review, you receive a quotation with deliverables, milestones, fees, third-party costs and an estimated schedule. Start dates depend on scope, approvals and access.

How do reporting and handover work?

The proposal defines the reporting frequency, agreed metrics, account ownership, file access and support arrangements. Ask for these before approving the scope.
