Privacy notice

How information submitted through this website is used.

Updated 12 November 2026. This notice covers enquiries, customer accounts and visa documents submitted to Sky Bridge FZE LLC through this website.

What we collect

Enquiries contain the name, contact details, company, service and message you provide. Visa requests can also contain nationality, residency, traveler details and documents you choose to upload. We do not collect payment card details on this site.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond, prepare quotations, coordinate requested services, manage applications and maintain necessary records. Form consent is for contact about your request; it does not subscribe you to marketing.

Storage and service providers

Website records and private document storage use Supabase. Email notifications use Resend when configured. These providers may process data outside the UAE. Staff access is restricted by role. Uploaded documents are not published as public files. Do not send passport scans through the general enquiry form.

Sharing

For a service you choose to proceed with, relevant information may need to be shared with the authority, licensed provider or attraction operator involved. Ask us who will handle your documents before proceeding. We do not sell submitted enquiry data.

Cookies and account storage

The customer portal uses browser storage for sign-in sessions. Enquiry forms retain a request identifier while you complete or retry a submission. External fonts and images may send normal network information to their providers. This version does not add advertising tracking or marketing cookies.

Retention and your choices

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, withdraw enquiry consent, or ask how long a specific record must be retained. Some records may need to be kept for service delivery, disputes or applicable obligations. Do not upload documents for another person without their permission.
