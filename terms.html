Service terms & cancellations

Understand the next step before committing to a service.

Updated 12 November 2026. The website presents available assistance and accepts service enquiries. Submitting an enquiry does not confirm a booking, visa application with an authority, advertising campaign or paid engagement.

Written scope and pricing

Before proceeding, obtain a written quotation describing the responsible provider, deliverables, currency, taxes where applicable, government or operator charges, service charges and payment terms. Indicative catalogue prices are not a final quotation. No online payment is collected on this website.

Visa assistance

Entry conditions depend on nationality, residence, purpose and current official rules. Assistance does not guarantee approval, entry or a processing date. The period for using a visa to enter a country and the permitted stay after entry are different; confirm both in the written service details. Arrival cards are not visas.

Experiences and tickets

Availability and final prices must be confirmed for your chosen date, time, ticket category and guests. Age rules, operating hours and inclusions vary by operator. A request or reference number is not an admission ticket.

Technology and advertising

Project milestones, support, revisions, intellectual property, account access and handover are set out in the agreed scope. Advertising media budgets and management fees are separate unless the written quotation expressly combines them. No fixed ranking, sales volume or return is guaranteed.

Cancellations, changes and refunds

Ask for the cancellation and refund conditions in writing before payment. Rules depend on the service, work already performed and third-party commitments. Government fees and issued tickets may have different restrictions. This page does not promise a universal refund or remove rights available under applicable law.
