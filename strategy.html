DELIVERABLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Know what your project includes.

Business and funnel review

Review the offer, current customer journey and business constraints. Identify whether the largest issue is traffic, conversion, follow-up or retention.

Prioritized channel roadmap

Recommend a practical sequence of channels and improvements. State what to do first, what depends on it and what evidence would change the plan.

Media and delivery budget

Separate media spend from production, management and development costs. Allocate budgets around the agreed objectives and testing priorities.

Measurement and review plan

Choose meaningful business indicators and agree how often to review them. Document assumptions and decision points for scaling or changing direction.

BEFORE WE START

Common questions.

Can I start with a strategy project before ongoing management?

Yes, request a separately scoped strategy engagement. The proposal will state the research, roadmap and handover included, with no assumption of ongoing campaign management.

What do you need from us?

Share your business website, target market, objectives, current setup and any constraints. Access is requested only for the accounts needed for the agreed scope.

When can work start, and what does it cost?

After the discovery review, you receive a quotation with deliverables, milestones, fees, third-party costs and an estimated schedule. Start dates depend on scope, approvals and access.

How do reporting and handover work?

The proposal defines the reporting frequency, agreed metrics, account ownership, file access and support arrangements. Ask for these before approving the scope.
