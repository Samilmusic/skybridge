DELIVERABLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Know what your project includes.

Customer journey and page plan

Map the information a visitor needs before taking action. Agree page count, content responsibilities, languages and the main conversion journey.

Responsive design and build

Build layouts that work across screen sizes with readable content, keyboard access and a coherent brand. Agree design reviews before development milestones.

Forms and integration testing

Test enquiries, bookings or ecommerce functions in the agreed scope, including error states and notification handling. Do not count a decorative form as a completed integration.

Launch and handover scope

Document domain, hosting and account ownership. The proposal sets launch checks, included support, ongoing maintenance and handover deliverables.

BEFORE WE START

Common questions.

Who owns the website and accounts?

Ownership, licensing and handover are specified in your agreement. Domain, hosting, third-party subscriptions and any ongoing maintenance charges should be stated before work starts.

What do you need from us?

Share your business website, target market, objectives, current setup and any constraints. Access is requested only for the accounts needed for the agreed scope.

When can work start, and what does it cost?

After the discovery review, you receive a quotation with deliverables, milestones, fees, third-party costs and an estimated schedule. Start dates depend on scope, approvals and access.

How do reporting and handover work?

The proposal defines the reporting frequency, agreed metrics, account ownership, file access and support arrangements. Ask for these before approving the scope.
